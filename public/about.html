Our Story

A Little Sweetness in Every Bite

"Peachy Maja was created to share the creamy, authentic, and delightful taste of homemade Maja Blanca. We aim to provide dessert lovers with an easy pre-ordering experience, sweet affordable treats, and a simple way to satisfy cravings."

Our Philosophy

Authentic Homemade Quality at ₱15

Maja Blanca is one of the Philippines' most cherished traditional desserts—rich coconut milk simmered to silky perfection with sweet whole corn kernels, chilled, and garnished with aromatic toasted latik and savory cheese.

At Peachy Maja, we prepare every batch fresh each morning. To ensure quality and avoid food waste, we operate on a pre-order basis, offering our signature single-serve Maja Blanca for only ₱15 per order.
